# Scene IR 0.12.0

Published. Do not edit. Wire version `"0.2"`.

Minor bump below 1.0 for a new capability contract. Documents still declare `irVersion` `"0.2"` and gate the new feature with `requiredCapabilities` containing `space.planar25d`. A package that does not enable that capability rejects the scene at accept time; older validators reject the unknown `spatialLayers` key.

## Capabilities introduced

- space.planar25d

## Representation

Optional scene field `spatialLayers: SpatialLayerDefinition[]`, also allowed on the `CREATE_SCENE` header (it is a base definition, frozen after the first seal). It aligns with the `@gvr/runtime` `SpatialLayer` shape: surfaces are listed on the layer; they are not discovered from groups.

- `SpatialLayerDefinition`: `{ id, camera, surfaces }`. `id` is a non-empty identifier, unique across layers.
- `SpatialCameraDefinition`: `{ projection: "perspective" | "orthographic", position?, yaw?, pitch?, roll?, focalLength?, zoom?, near? }`. Positions are `{ x, y, z }`; angles are radians; every number is finite, and `focalLength`, `zoom` and `near` are greater than 0 when present.
- `SpatialSurfaceDefinition`: `{ nodeId, parent?, transform?, backFill?, backface? }`. `nodeId` names a root-scene node and appears in at most one surface of the scene. `parent` names another surface of the same layer; parents may not form a cycle. `backFill` is a solid colour; `backface` is `"visible"` (default) or `"hidden"`.
- `SpatialTransformDefinition`: `{ position?, pivot?, rotation?, scale?, hinge? }` with `{ x, y, z }` vectors. `SpatialHingeDefinition`: `{ from, to, angle }` with 2D `from` and `to` points that differ and a finite radian `angle`.
- A scene holds at most 1024 surfaces across all layers. Unknown keys on layer, camera, surface, transform, hinge and vector objects are rejected.

Validation: a non-empty `spatialLayers` requires `space.planar25d`.

## Sampling

Frame sampling and `ResolvedFrame` are unchanged. A host projects the resolved frame with the layers at frame resolution (`projectSpatialFrame`), per `docs/decisions/engine/spatial-graphics.md`. A scene without `spatialLayers` samples exactly as in 0.11.0.

## Readers

A 0.12 consumer accepts wire `"0.2"` documents. Migrated 0.1 scenes have no spatial layers.

## Not in this release

Animated spatial channels, surfaces for composition nodes, per-node `surface` fields, and spatial ops in `APPLY_ENTITY_PATCH`. Other pending ids remain outside this file.
