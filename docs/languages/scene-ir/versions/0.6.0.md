# Scene IR 0.6.0

Published. Do not edit. Wire version `"0.2"`.

Minor bump below 1.0 for a new capability contract. Documents still declare `irVersion` `"0.2"` and gate the new feature with `requiredCapabilities` containing `entities.durableRevisions`. A package that does not enable that capability rejects `APPLY_ENTITY_PATCH` at admission.

## Capabilities introduced

- entities.durableRevisions

## Representation

`RuntimeOperation` may include `APPLY_ENTITY_PATCH` with a revision-guarded `EntityPatch`. Patches upsert/remove/reparent entities, grant or revoke exclusive channel ownership, and activate or cancel named behaviours. Optional `node` payloads on `UPSERT_ENTITY` bind drawable geometry to the entity id. Activation at or after the sealed horizon is allowed and remains invisible to the sealed left-limit sample; activation before the seal is rejected (`SEALED_HISTORY` / `INVALID_TIMING`). Accepted patches do not rewrite presented sealed history.

`ADD_NODES` remains frozen after the first seal. Post-seal topology changes use entity patches.

## Readers

A 0.6 consumer accepts wire `"0.2"` documents. Durable entity patches require `entities.durableRevisions` in `requiredCapabilities`. Migrated 0.1 scenes do not gain entity patches.

## Not in this release

Other pending ids remain outside this file. Adapter coverage is in the registry. Drawable reuse / channel bindings across shared geometry remain E005.
