# MotionScript 0.11.0

Published. Do not edit. Wire version `"0.2"`.

Minor bump below 1.0 for a new capability contract. Programs still declare `version "0.2"` and gate the new feature with `requires "audio.cues"` (the compiler adds it when an `audio` or `cue` declaration lowers). A package that does not enable that capability rejects the document at accept time. Decision: `docs/decisions/engine/audible-media-clock.md` (E008); implementation E026.

## Capabilities introduced

- audio.cues

## Syntax

`audio` and `cue` are the only new reserved words. Bus names and cue body words (`narration`, `effects`, `music`, `gain`, `loops`, `fadeIn`, `fadeOut`, `required`, …) are matched by spelling and stay usable as identifiers elsewhere. (`offset` and `duration` were already reserved.)

```
audio Voice "lesson/voice.wav" 4.2s;
audio Click "ui/click.wav" 120ms;
audio Bed "music/bed.wav" 8s;
cue Intro narration Voice at 0ms;
cue Tap effects Click at 1.2s { gain 0.8; };
cue Music music Bed at 0ms { offset 100ms; duration 4s; loops 3; gain 0.5; fadeIn 200ms; fadeOut 300ms; required false; };
```

- `audio <id> "<href>" <length>;` declares a resource with its decoded length. Readiness is reported by the host loader at playback; the program declares none. `audio` is a base definition: frozen after the first `seal`.
- `cue <id> <bus> <resource> at <time> [{ … }];` schedules one cue on root scene time. Bus is `narration`, `effects` or `music`. Body properties: `offset`, `duration`, `fadeIn`, `fadeOut` (times), `loops` (integer 1..1000), `gain` (0..4), `required` (`true`/`false`; default true for narration, false otherwise).
- A cue may follow the first `seal` when it starts at or after the sealed horizon; earlier starts are `INVALID_TIMING`. Forward references to a later `audio` wait; unresolved resources report `UNKNOWN_TARGET`. Duplicate resource or cue ids are `DUPLICATE_ID`.

## Semantics

Scene time is the master clock. Playback, ducking, required-resource holds, loop/seek/rate behaviour, triggered one-shots and offline export follow the E008 decision and the `@gvr/runtime` `AudioSession` / `mixAudio` contract.

## Readers

A 0.11 consumer accepts wire `"0.2"` documents. Audio requires `audio.cues` in `requires`. Older packages reject `audio` / `cue` as unexpected declarations. Programs without them compile exactly as in 0.10.0, including identifiers spelled like bus or cue body words.

## Not in this release

Speech synthesis/recognition, spatial/positional audio, ducking ramps, per-cue rate, timing-scope-relative cues and audio triggered from `interact` bindings. Other pending ids remain outside this file.
