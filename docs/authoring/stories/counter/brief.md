# Counter: brief

Direction script as supplied, recorded 2026-10-29.

---

**STICK FIGHT — “COUNTER”**

**Duration:** 30 seconds  
**Style:** Fast, exaggerated 2D action. No dialogue or gore.  
**Stage:** Flat floor, dark background, one vertical pillar on the right.  
**Fighters:** Cyan is quick and evasive. Orange is powerful and relentless.

**0:00–0:03 — The challenge**

Wide shot. Cyan stands left, Orange stands right.

Orange cracks their neck and raises both fists. Cyan casually beckons with two fingers.

Hold for half a second.

Orange immediately lunges.

*Sound: quiet room tone → sharp accelerating whoosh.*

**0:03–0:07 — Three attacks, three answers**

Orange throws a straight punch. Cyan leans back; the fist passes just in front of their face.

Orange follows with a low sweeping kick. Cyan hops over it, knees tucked.

As Cyan lands, Orange swings an overhead hammer fist. Cyan sidesteps. Orange’s fist strikes the floor, sending out a small shock ring.

Cyan looks at the crater, then at Orange.

*Choreography: make each dodge smaller than the last. Cyan appears completely in control.*

**0:07–0:11 — The reversal**

Cyan taps Orange on the shoulder.

Orange turns into a quick jab, then takes a second punch to the torso. Cyan winds up an exaggerated finishing punch—

Orange catches the fist.

Brief stillness.

Orange pulls Cyan forward and shoulder-checks them across the stage. Cyan skids backward, heels scraping, stopping just before the pillar.

*Sound: tap, pop, pop → silence → heavy thud and scraping.*

**0:11–0:16 — The pillar exchange**

Orange charges with a flying knee.

Cyan ducks. Orange’s knee hits the pillar, cracking it.

Cyan plants one foot against the pillar and springs into a backward somersault over Orange. As Cyan passes overhead, Orange turns and swings upward, narrowly missing.

Cyan lands behind Orange in a low crouch.

Both turn toward each other.

*Camera: pan right with the charge; pull back enough to keep the entire flip visible.*

**0:16–0:21 — Close combat**

Cyan advances:

- Left jab — Orange blocks outward.
- Right cross — Orange ducks.
- Turning kick — Orange catches Cyan’s ankle.

Orange swings Cyan toward the floor. Cyan plants both hands, bends through the landing, and kicks Orange’s wrist with the free leg.

The grip breaks.

Cyan completes the cartwheel and lands. Orange stumbles one step. Both reset their guards.

*Choreography: keep the caught leg visibly connected to Orange’s hand until the release.*

**0:21–0:25 — The feint**

Orange lowers their stance and draws back a huge right punch.

Cyan braces for another dodge.

Orange starts forward—but stops short.

Cyan dodges early.

Orange grins and redirects into a sweeping left hook. Cyan barely raises both forearms in time. The impact launches Cyan backward.

Cyan lands in a crouch, one hand dragging across the floor.

For the first time, Cyan looks serious.

**0:25–0:28 — The final clash**

Both sprint toward centre stage.

Orange commits to a straight punch. Cyan slides underneath it, plants one hand, and pivots into a rising kick.

The kick connects with Orange’s torso.

**Impact pose:** Cyan extended diagonally upward; Orange folded around the hit.

Hold the silhouette for three frames. Add a single white flash and a tight camera shake.

Orange flies backward and lands seated against the cracked pillar.

**0:28–0:30 — The punchline**

Cyan stands, dusts off their hands, and offers Orange a hand.

Orange reaches up.

Behind Orange, the pillar gives a tiny creak.

Both slowly look upward.

Cut to black.

*Sound: one small falling pebble … then a tremendous crash.*

**Animation direction**

Keep Cyan on the left and Orange on the right except during the clearly visible pillar flip. Give major attacks a readable wind-up, accelerate into contact, then allow recovery. Use sharp impact poses and brief pauses between exchanges so the speed remains understandable.
