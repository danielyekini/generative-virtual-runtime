# From MotionScript to Motion: brief

Direction script as supplied, recorded 2026-10-29.

---

Title: From MotionScript to Motion
Duration: 30 seconds
Style and tone: Simple 2D motion graphics. Dark background, crisp labels, cyan code, violet scene data, amber timing signals, and white rendered objects.
Setting and initial layout: A horizontal pipeline spans the scene: MotionScript → Compiler → Scene IR → Runtime → Renderer. A preview canvas sits beneath the final two stages.
Characters or subjects: One white circle remains the example throughout. Travelling highlights show information passing between stages.

[0–5s] — Describe the animation
- Camera: Hold on a close-up of the MotionScript panel.
- Action and poses: Three readable instruction cards appear sequentially: “Create a circle,” “Move right over two seconds,” and “Ease in and out.” A small label identifies MotionScript as the animation authoring language.
- Timing: Each card enters over 0.4 seconds; hold the completed description for two seconds.
- Sound and effects: Three soft typing clicks.
- Transition: The cards compress into a cyan document and travel right toward the compiler.
- Narration: “MotionScript describes the scene, its objects, and how they change over time.”

[5–10s] — Compile the description
- Camera: Track the document right; stop with the compiler centred.
- Action and poses: The compiler opens the document into organised rows. Highlights inspect its structure, object references, and animation instructions. The rows then assemble into a violet package labelled Scene IR.
- Timing: Inspection takes three seconds; assembly takes one second; hold for one second.
- Sound and effects: Gentle scanning pulses followed by a confirmation chime.
- Transition: The violet package expands into the next shot.
- Narration: “The compiler checks those instructions and converts them into a structured scene representation.”

[10–15s] — Organise the scene
- Camera: Hold on the expanded Scene IR.
- Action and poses: Three connected cards settle into place: Objects, Animation tracks, and Camera. The object card contains the circle; the animation card shows its position changing along a timeline. A bracket groups all three beneath “Scene IR.”
- Timing: Cards appear during the first two seconds; hold the complete arrangement for three seconds.
- Sound and effects: Quiet placement clicks.
- Transition: The timeline extends right into the runtime panel.
- Narration: “Scene IR stores what exists, what animates, and the camera settings in one common format.”

[15–21s] — Evaluate time
- Camera: Widen to show the runtime above the preview canvas.
- Action and poses: A playhead advances from zero to two seconds. Simultaneously, a runtime marker follows an easing curve, and the circle’s evaluated position advances along a horizontal guide. Labels appear at the start, midpoint, and end: Start, Between, Finish.
- Timing: Run the two-second movement, pause, then scrub back to its midpoint. The evaluated position returns with the playhead.
- Sound and effects: A steady, quiet clock pulse.
- Transition: The midpoint state travels as an amber packet into the renderer.
- Narration: “At a chosen time, the runtime evaluates the animation tracks to determine the scene’s current state.”

[21–26s] — Draw the result
- Camera: Push into the renderer and its preview canvas.
- Action and poses: The renderer receives the evaluated state. The circle appears at the corresponding position on the canvas. Successive state packets arrive as playback resumes; the circle moves smoothly right, slowing into its final position.
- Timing: Hold the first drawn frame for one second; complete the movement over two seconds; hold the result for two seconds.
- Sound and effects: Soft pulses accompany incoming states.
- Transition: Pull back to reveal the full pipeline.
- Narration: “The renderer draws that state onto the canvas. Repeating this as time advances produces the animation.”

[26–30s] — Connect the whole system
- Camera: Hold a wide view of all five stages.
- Action and poses: A single highlight travels from MotionScript through the compiler, Scene IR, runtime, and renderer. A bracket beneath the pipeline reads GVR: the system that turns animation descriptions into visuals. The completed circle remains visible below.
- Timing: Highlight travels for two seconds; final composition holds for two seconds.
- Sound and effects: A soft resolving tone.
- Transition: End on the complete pipeline.
- Narration: “MotionScript expresses the animation. GVR compiles, evaluates, and renders it.”

Final composition: The full pipeline remains readable, with the white circle resting at its final position on the preview canvas.
