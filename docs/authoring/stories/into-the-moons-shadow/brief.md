# Into the Moon’s Shadow: brief

Direction script as supplied, recorded 2026-10-29.

---

**Title:** Into the Moon’s Shadow  
**Duration:** 40 seconds  
**Style and tone:** Stylised scientific illustration. Warm ivory Sun, slate Moon, blue Earth, and translucent shadow regions. Restrained labels and smooth, deliberate motion.

**Setting and initial layout:** Two simultaneous panels: an orbital overview above and an Earth observer’s sky below. The Sun sits left, the Moon between Sun and Earth, and Earth right. A marked observer lies on the approaching umbral track. Display **“Not to scale — sizes, distances and timing exaggerated”** throughout.

**Characters or subjects:** Sun, Moon, Earth, the Moon’s dark **umbra** and lighter **penumbra**, and one observer marked by an orange dot. Choose an alignment in which the umbra reaches Earth and the Moon’s apparent disc fully covers the Sun at totality.

**[0:00–0:05] — Two connected viewpoints**

- **Camera:** Establish both panels. Slowly move closer to the orbital overview while keeping the observer panel fixed.
- **Action and poses:** Label Sun, Moon, and Earth. Highlight the observer on Earth’s daylight side. In the lower panel, show an unobstructed solar disc, labelled **“Observer’s sky — filtered view outside totality.”**
- **Timing:** Reveal labels sequentially over three seconds; hold the relationship for two seconds.
- **Sound and effects:** Quiet ambient tone; no sound implying travel through space.
- **Transition:** Continue the overview movement as the Moon approaches alignment.

**[0:05–0:10] — Build the shadow**

- **Camera:** Settle into a side-on overview that clearly shows the shadow intersecting Earth.
- **Action and poses:** Reveal boundary rays from the Sun’s edges past the Moon. Fill the central narrowing cone as **“Umbra: Sun fully hidden”** and the surrounding region as **“Penumbra: Sun partly hidden.”** Show the umbral cone reaching Earth before its tip. The observer remains outside both regions, so the lower Sun stays unobstructed.
- **Timing:** Draw rays first, then reveal shadow fills and labels.
- **Sound and effects:** Soft label ticks. Keep objects visible through the translucent penumbra.
- **Transition:** Begin the shadow’s continuous sweep across Earth.

**[0:10–0:15] — Partial eclipse begins**

- **Camera:** Track the shadow footprint across Earth. Keep the Sun–Moon–Earth alignment visible.
- **Action and poses:** At 0:11, the penumbra’s leading boundary reaches the observer. Simultaneously, the Moon’s silhouette first touches the Sun’s edge in the lower panel. As the observer moves deeper into penumbra relative to the shadow, the silhouette advances across the solar disc. Label **“Partial eclipse.”**
- **Timing:** Make first contact simultaneous in both panels; continue steadily afterward.
- **Sound and effects:** Subtle contact cue. Keep the uncovered solar surface uniformly bright in the filtered view.
- **Transition:** Start enlarging the Earth region while retaining a small full-system inset.

**[0:15–0:20] — Approaching the umbra**

- **Camera:** Zoom the overview into Earth’s curved surface and the approaching umbral footprint. Preserve the original overview in an inset.
- **Action and poses:** Keep the observer dot anchored to Earth’s surface. The umbral boundary approaches it while the observer’s Sun narrows to a thin crescent. Reveal a narrow **“Path of totality”** on the surface beneath the moving umbra. Gradually dim the observer’s surrounding sky.
- **Timing:** Ease the zoom to a stop by 0:18. Maintain continuous eclipse progression.
- **Sound and effects:** Ambient tone softens. Avoid revealing a full corona while the bright crescent remains.
- **Transition:** Hold both views as the umbral edge reaches the observer.

**[0:20–0:25] — Entering totality**

- **Camera:** Hold the enlarged Earth view above and the observer’s sky below.
- **Action and poses:** At exactly 0:21, the observer crosses inside the umbra and the last solar crescent disappears in the lower panel. Change the label to **“Total eclipse.”** Reveal the delicate corona around the Moon’s black disc. Add **“Corona shown during totality”** as the observer view changes from its filtered presentation.
- **Timing:** Synchronize umbral entry and complete solar coverage precisely. Hold totality through the end of the beat.
- **Sound and effects:** A gentle tonal change marks entry. Reveal the corona with a restrained fade.
- **Transition:** Continue moving the shadow footprint while keeping the observer inside it.

**[0:25–0:30] — Inside the shadow**

- **Camera:** Slowly widen the upper view to show the dark umbral footprint nested within the larger penumbra.
- **Action and poses:** The observer remains inside the umbra. In the lower panel, the Moon continues moving across the Sun while still covering its entire bright disc. Briefly display **“Inside umbra → total eclipse”** and **“Inside penumbra → partial eclipse.”**
- **Timing:** Maintain the same continuous motion; avoid freezing the Moon during totality. Remove explanatory text by 0:29.
- **Sound and effects:** Quiet sustained ambience. Keep the corona subtle and stable.
- **Transition:** The trailing umbral boundary approaches the observer.

**[0:30–0:35] — Leaving totality**

- **Camera:** Hold both panels steady so the paired event is easy to compare.
- **Action and poses:** At exactly 0:31, the observer leaves the umbra and a bright sliver of Sun reappears on the opposite side from first contact. Return the lower panel to its labelled filtered view and remove the corona. Change the stage label to **“Partial eclipse.”** The observer remains inside the penumbra as the visible solar crescent grows.
- **Timing:** Synchronize umbral exit and returning sunlight. Restore sky brightness gradually.
- **Sound and effects:** Soft transition cue; no exaggerated flare.
- **Transition:** Pull the upper camera back toward the full orbital overview.

**[0:35–0:40] — Eclipse ends**

- **Camera:** Complete the pullback, restoring the opening two-panel composition.
- **Action and poses:** At 0:38, the trailing penumbral boundary clears the observer exactly as the Moon’s silhouette leaves the solar disc. Label the lower panel **“Eclipse ended.”** Hold the unobstructed Sun while the upper view shows the shadow continuing beyond the observer.
- **Timing:** Complete all motion needed for final contact by 0:38; hold for two seconds.
- **Sound and effects:** Ambient tone resolves softly.
- **Transition:** End without a fade, retaining the scale disclaimer.

**Final visible composition:** The orbital overview shows the shadow moving away from the marked observer; the simultaneous sky view shows the full Sun again.

**Continuity rule:** Drive both panels from the same relative positions and eclipse timeline. Keep the sky view’s orientation fixed: first contact and final contact occur on opposite solar limbs. Camera movement must never change the physical alignment or shift the observer independently across Earth.
