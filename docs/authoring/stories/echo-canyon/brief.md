# ECHO Canyon: brief

Director script as supplied, recorded 2026-10-29.

---

Title: ECHO Canyon
Duration: 20 seconds
Style and tone: Kinetic typography with bold condensed lettering. Warm sandstone canyon walls, a dark violet interior, and cream text. Successive echoes become smaller, cooler, and more transparent.
Setting and initial layout: A stylised canyon forms a deep V-shaped corridor. Its walls frame a clear central passage, narrowing toward a distant opening.
Characters or subjects: The word ECHO, followed by five repetitions. Keep every word upright and readable; use position, scale, and slight rotation to convey ricochets.
[0–4s] — Send the call

* Camera: Wide view looking into the canyon; hold still.
* Action and poses: ECHO expands into the foreground from the lower centre. Its letters compress together briefly, then spring forward as one word toward the right wall. A soft elliptical shadow separates from the text as it crosses the canyon.
* Timing: Reveal during 0–1s; anticipation squeeze during 1–1.5s; accelerate toward the wall during 1.5–3.5s; contact at 3.5s.
* Sound and effects: One clear spoken “Echo!” A thin curved pulse radiates from the word.
* Transition: At contact, flatten the word slightly against the wall before its direction reverses.

[4–8s] — First reflection

* Camera: Begin a slow push into the canyon.
* Action and poses: The original word remains briefly as a faint imprint at the right wall. A new ECHO, at 75% scale and 75% opacity, rebounds diagonally left and deeper into the canyon. Keep the letters facing the viewer. At 7s, it contacts the left wall, compresses along its travel direction, and releases a small ripple.
* Timing: Rebound quickly, then ease slightly before contact. Hold the wall imprint for 0.5s before fading it.
* Sound and effects: First delayed “Echo,” quieter and more reverberant.
* Transition: The next repetition emerges from the left-wall contact point.

[8–12s] — Across and farther away

* Camera: Continue the gentle forward push, keeping both canyon walls visible.
* Action and poses: A second repetition, at 55% scale and 52% opacity, travels toward the right wall farther into the canyon. At 10.5s, it makes contact. A third repetition, at 38% scale and 34% opacity, rebounds leftward from that same point. Previous words linger only as fading traces.
* Timing: Second repetition crosses during 8–10.5s; third begins immediately after contact and continues through 12s.
* Sound and effects: Two increasingly quiet echoes aligned with their departures. Contact ripples shrink with the text.
* Transition: Follow the third repetition toward a distant left-wall ledge.

[12–16s] — Echoes recede

* Camera: Ease the push to a stop; let the shrinking typography create the remaining depth.
* Action and poses: The third repetition reaches the left wall at 12.5s. A fourth ECHO, at 25% scale and 20% opacity, rebounds rightward. It reaches the opposite wall at 15s, releasing the fifth and final repetition at 15% scale and 10% opacity. This last word travels toward the distant central opening.
* Timing: Keep each new word continuous with its contact point. Reduce bounce deformation until the final repetition barely flexes.
* Sound and effects: Two soft, diffuse echoes; the last trails into reverberation.
* Transition: All nearer word traces disappear, leaving only the final distant repetition.

[16–20s] — Into silence

* Camera: Hold the canyon composition.
* Action and poses: The final ECHO drifts toward the opening, shrinking from 15% to 8% scale and fading to zero by 18.5s. Its final ripple expands gently and vanishes. Hold the empty canyon for the remaining 1.5 seconds.
* Timing: Slow, continuous recession with no final bounce.
* Sound and effects: Reverberation decays into silence by 19s.
* Transition: End on the still canyon.

Final composition: Empty sandstone walls frame the distant opening. No typography remains; the final visual and audible echo have fully faded.
