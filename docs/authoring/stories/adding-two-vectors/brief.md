# Adding Two Vectors: brief

Direction script as supplied, recorded 2026-10-29.

---

Title: Adding Two Vectors
Duration: 30 seconds
Style and tone: Clean educational diagrams on an off-white background. Dark grey labels, muted blue vector a, muted amber vector b, and teal resultant a + b. Consistent arrowheads and clear typography.

Setting and initial layout: A faint square grid with an origin O near the lower left. Keep equal horizontal and vertical scales. Use a = (3, 1) and b = (1, 2) throughout.

Characters or subjects: Labelled vector arrows, endpoint markers, coordinate guides, and one component equation.

[0:00–0:05] — Meet the vectors
- Camera: Fixed front-facing view of the diagram; hold this framing throughout.
- Action and poses: Reveal the grid and origin. Draw blue a from O to (3, 1), then amber b from O to (1, 2). Place each label beside its arrow. Display “Add vectors by placing them head to tail.”
- Timing: Draw a during 0:01–0:02 and b during 0:02.5–0:03.5. Hold both for the remaining time.
- Sound and effects: Optional soft ticks as each arrow finishes.
- Transition: Dim the instruction slightly while keeping both arrows visible.

[0:05–0:10] — Move without changing
- Camera: Fixed.
- Action and poses: Translate b as one rigid arrow until its tail meets the head of a at (3, 1). Its head arrives at (4, 3). Leave a faint dashed copy at its original position. Display “Same length. Same direction.”
- Timing: Move during 0:05.5–0:08 with gentle easing; hold through 0:10.
- Sound and effects: Optional quiet click when the endpoints align. Briefly outline the shared endpoint.
- Transition: Fade the dashed original copy and explanatory text.

[0:10–0:15] — Draw the sum
- Camera: Fixed.
- Action and poses: Mark the starting point O and final endpoint (4, 3). Draw a teal arrow directly between them, labelled a + b. Keep the blue and amber arrows visible as the two-step route. Display “The sum connects the start to the final endpoint.”
- Timing: Mark endpoints during 0:10–0:11; draw the resultant during 0:11–0:13; hold for two seconds.
- Sound and effects: Optional soft resolving tone when the resultant reaches its endpoint.
- Transition: Reduce the route arrows’ emphasis slightly while retaining their colours and labels.

[0:15–0:20] — Add the components
- Camera: Fixed; reserve the right side for the equation.
- Action and poses: Reveal faint dashed guides from (4, 3) to the axes. Show the equation in three stages:
  a + b = (3, 1) + (1, 2)
  = (3 + 1, 1 + 2)
  = (4, 3)
  Briefly underline the horizontal terms together, then the vertical terms.
- Timing: Reveal each equation stage about one second apart. Hold the completed calculation from 0:18–0:20.
- Sound and effects: Optional light ticks with each equation stage.
- Transition: Fade the coordinate guides and intermediate calculation lines; retain a + b = (4, 3).

[0:20–0:25] — Reverse the order
- Camera: Fixed.
- Action and poses: Keep the teal resultant stationary. Fade the coloured route to a faint reference. Draw amber b from O to (1, 2), then blue a from there to (4, 3). Both routes now reach the same marked endpoint. Display “Either order reaches the same endpoint.”
- Timing: Draw b during 0:20.5–0:21.5 and a during 0:22–0:23. Hold the comparison through 0:25.
- Sound and effects: Optional matching ticks as the arrows finish. Give the shared endpoint one restrained outline pulse.
- Transition: Fade the original faint route and replace the explanatory sentence with the final rule.

[0:25–0:30] — Hold the principle
- Camera: Fixed.
- Action and poses: Retain the reversed head-to-tail route and the teal resultant. Display a + b = b + a, followed beneath by “Place head to tail. Connect start to finish.” Keep a + b = (4, 3) in the equation area.
- Timing: Complete all text changes by 0:27. Hold the finished diagram motionless for the last three seconds.
- Sound and effects: None.
- Transition: End on the fully visible diagram without a fade.

Final visible composition: Two coloured arrows form a head-to-tail route from O to (4, 3). The teal resultant connects those endpoints directly, with the component sum and order rule clearly readable alongside.
