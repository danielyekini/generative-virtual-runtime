# Brief as supplied — 2026-10-29

**Title:** Threads into Motion  
**Duration:** 30 seconds  
**Style and tone:** Textured graphic animation on warm ivory. Indigo warp threads, coral weft thread, visible fibres, and soft contact shadows. The final ribbon preserves the coral colour and texture.  
**Setting and initial layout:** A square weaving area floats centrally, with twelve vertical indigo threads stretched between two slim wooden bars. One continuous coral thread feeds from the right.  
**Characters or subjects:** Indigo warp threads, a coral weft thread with a small shuttle at its leading end, and the ribbon it gradually becomes. Preserve alternating over-under crossings and continuous thread identity.

**[0–5s] — Stretch the threads**

- **Camera:** Close overhead view; slowly widen to reveal the square weaving area.
- **Action and poses:** Twelve indigo threads extend downward from the upper bar and attach to the lower bar. Each straightens under tension with a brief sideways vibration. The coral shuttle enters from the right and pauses beside the bottom of the warp.
- **Timing:** Threads extend in a staggered sequence during 0–3s; vibrations settle during 3–4s; shuttle arrives during 4–5s.
- **Sound and effects:** Soft fibre rustles and delicate plucked notes.
- **Transition:** Push closer to the first weaving row.

**[5–11s] — Establish the weave**

- **Camera:** Slightly angled macro view so crossings and contact shadows remain visible.
- **Action and poses:** Alternate warp threads lift into a shallow opening. The shuttle travels right to left through it, laying the coral thread across the warp. The lifted threads lower, trapping the first row. At the left edge, the coral thread curves around the outermost warp. The opposite warp threads lift, and the shuttle returns left to right, reversing the crossing pattern. A slim comb presses both rows together.
- **Timing:** First pass takes two seconds; edge turn and warp exchange take one second; return pass takes two seconds; compression takes one second.
- **Sound and effects:** Dry sliding fibres and a soft wooden comb tap.
- **Transition:** Return smoothly to an overhead view while the shuttle continues moving.

**[11–16s] — Grow the fabric**

- **Camera:** Widen until the entire weaving area is visible.
- **Action and poses:** Accelerate the repeated weaving cycle. The shuttle alternates direction, forming continuous loops at the edges. Rows pack upward from the bottom into a square fabric swatch. Keep alternating crossings visible along the advancing edge. As the square fills, add fine fibre fuzz and small irregularities in spacing.
- **Timing:** Begin at the established pace, accelerate during 11–13s, then maintain the faster rhythm. The final row settles at 16s.
- **Sound and effects:** Rhythmic swishes and taps build into a light percussive pattern.
- **Transition:** The shuttle stops at the upper-right corner; the comb retreats.

**[16–20s] — Feel the woven surface**

- **Camera:** Glide across the fabric at a shallow angle, then return overhead.
- **Action and poses:** A gentle ripple travels from the lower-left to upper-right, bending both thread systems together. Moving shadows reveal the raised crossings. The ripple settles. At 19s, the shuttle detaches from the coral thread’s exposed upper-right end and slides out of frame. That loose end lifts and curls.
- **Timing:** Surface ripple takes two seconds; camera returns overhead during the third second; loose end rises during the final second.
- **Sound and effects:** Soft cloth movement; percussion falls away.
- **Transition:** Follow the lifted coral end without cutting.

**[20–25s] — Unweave the square**

- **Camera:** Hold the fabric and growing loose thread together; widen gradually.
- **Action and poses:** The coral end pulls upward and right, withdrawing the final row through its crossings. Successive rows unzip in reverse weaving order, alternating direction. Each freed length joins one continuous flowing strand. The indigo warp remains stretched between the bars as the woven area shrinks from top to bottom. Once the last coral row clears, the indigo threads retract into the bars, which slide outward and leave the frame.
- **Timing:** First row releases slowly during 20–21s; unweaving accelerates during 21–24s; warp and bars exit during 24–25s.
- **Sound and effects:** Fine zipper-like fibre sounds, followed by a soft release.
- **Transition:** Centre the uninterrupted coral strand against the now-empty background.

**[25–30s] — Become a dancing ribbon**

- **Camera:** Follow the strand’s centre with a gentle pullback, keeping its full gesture in frame.
- **Action and poses:** The coral strand visibly flattens and widens, with the change travelling along its length from the leading end. Fine fibres spread into parallel striations across its new ribbon surface. The leading tip sweeps upward; the trailing length follows with a delayed S-curve. The ribbon makes one broad loop, twists to reveal a darker reverse face, then opens into a floating wave. At its single self-crossing, the nearer section clearly passes in front.
- **Timing:** Transformation takes 25–26.5s; loop and twist unfold during 26.5–29s; motion eases into the final wave during 29–30s.
- **Sound and effects:** Airy fabric swishes and a soft resolving note.
- **Transition:** End with a slight residual flutter.

**Final composition:** A coral ribbon hangs in a broad S-curve, its leading tip raised and trailing end curling below. Visible fibres and edge texture connect it to the thread that formed the fabric.
