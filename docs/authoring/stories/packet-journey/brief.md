# Packet Journey: brief

Direction script as supplied, recorded 2026-10-29.

---

Title: Packet Journey
Duration: 35 seconds
Style and tone: Dark technical schematic with fine grey connections and luminous cyan paths. Packets use restrained violet accents; amber marks waiting and retransmission. Labels remain crisp during camera movement.

Setting and initial layout: Sender on the left, receiver on the right, and three branching routes through intermediate routers. Beneath the receiver, a reassembly buffer contains five numbered slots. A compact sender-side timer tracks the missing packet.

Characters or subjects: One message divided into five packets, labelled 1–5. Each carries a distinct message fragment. Retransmitted packet 3 retains its original number and payload.

[0:00–0:05] — Message becomes packets
- Camera: Begin close on the sender, then pull back to reveal the whole network.
- Action and poses: A message bar labelled “HELLO, NETWORK!” separates into five fragments: “HEL”, “LO,”, “ NET”, “WOR”, “K!”. Each fragment becomes a packet tile numbered 1–5. Packets queue beside the sender as the branching links illuminate.
- Timing: Split during 0:01–0:03. Complete the network reveal by 0:05.
- Sound and effects: Five quiet digital ticks. Packet borders brighten once as their numbers appear.
- Transition: Maintain the wide view as the first packet enters the network.

[0:05–0:11] — Routes diverge
- Camera: Hold the whole network, keeping sender and receiver visible.
- Action and poses: Launch packets 1–5 at short intervals. Packet 1 follows the upper route and waits briefly at a busy router. Packets 2 and 4 take a faster middle route. Packet 3 enters the lower route; 5 follows the upper route behind 1. At 0:09, packet 3 reaches a failing link, flashes amber, and disappears. Mark that location “Packet lost”; other traffic continues.
- Timing: Use steady travel between routers and visible pauses inside them. Avoid overtaking on a single link.
- Sound and effects: Soft routing clicks. One muted glitch marks the loss. Active links glow only around passing packets.
- Transition: Pan and push toward the receiver while packet 4 approaches its input.

[0:11–0:17] — Out-of-order arrival
- Camera: Close view of the receiver and its five-slot buffer. Keep incoming links visible at the left edge.
- Action and poses: Packet 4 arrives at 0:11.5, 2 at 0:13, and 1 at 0:15. Each enters its matching numbered slot, leaving slots 3 and 5 empty. Show arrival history 4 → 2 → 1 above the buffer. The output remains labelled “Waiting for all packets.”
- Timing: Give each arrival a short intake motion, a clear slot placement, and a brief hold.
- Sound and effects: Three soft confirmation tones. Filled slots glow cyan; empty slots retain dashed outlines.
- Transition: Pull back enough to include the upper route and sender-side status.

[0:17–0:22] — A missing piece
- Camera: Widen to the whole network, with the receiver buffer still legible in an inset.
- Action and poses: Packet 5 completes the upper route and enters slot 5 at 0:18.5. Slots 1, 2, 4, 5 now hold fragments; 3 remains empty. Small acknowledgement signals return toward the sender for received packets. At the sender, highlight “3 — awaiting acknowledgement” beside its nearly expired timer.
- Timing: Hold the incomplete buffer for two seconds after packet 5 arrives.
- Sound and effects: One arrival tone. A quiet, slow tick accompanies the final timer segment.
- Transition: Shift framing slightly toward the sender as the timer reaches zero.

[0:22–0:27] — Timeout and retransmission
- Camera: Medium view of the sender and lower network, then track the replacement packet rightward.
- Action and poses: At 0:22, the timer reaches zero and displays “Timeout: packet 3.” The sender creates another copy from its retained data, labelled “3 — retransmission.” Launch it at 0:23.5 through the working middle route. Keep the failed lower link dim and marked with a small break symbol.
- Timing: Hold the timeout label for one second before launch. Let the retransmitted packet pause briefly at a router, then continue.
- Sound and effects: Restrained warning tone, followed by a launch click. Packet 3 carries an amber outline while travelling.
- Transition: Follow packet 3, then ease into a receiver close-up before it arrives.

[0:27–0:31] — Buffer complete
- Camera: Settle on the receiver, showing all five slots and the output area.
- Action and poses: Packet 3 arrives at 0:28 and enters the empty third slot. Its outline changes from amber to cyan. Only after it settles, replace “Waiting for all packets” with “All packets received.” Briefly highlight slots 1–5 in numerical order, showing the fragments in their correct sequence.
- Timing: Hold the complete buffer for one second before beginning the ordered highlight.
- Sound and effects: A confirmation tone on arrival, followed by five light sequential ticks.
- Transition: Move the ordered fragments downward toward the output area.

[0:31–0:35] — Message reconstructed
- Camera: Hold the receiver close-up through 0:33, then gently pull back to include the network.
- Action and poses: Join the five fragments into “HELLO, NETWORK!” at 0:32. Remove packet boundaries only after the complete text is assembled. Send a final acknowledgement toward the sender. Display “Message complete” beneath the reconstructed bar while the branching network dims behind it.
- Timing: Complete assembly by 0:32.5. Hold the readable message through 0:35.
- Sound and effects: Soft resolving chord. A single cyan sweep crosses the completed message.
- Transition: End on the held composition without a cut.

Final visible composition: The complete message glows beside the receiver, with all five numbered buffer slots filled beneath it. The network remains visible behind, including the failed link and the successful retransmission route.
