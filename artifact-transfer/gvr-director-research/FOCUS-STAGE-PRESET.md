# Proposed focus-stage preference

Status: user-endorsed aesthetic direction, not installed skill/schema or runtime feature. Keep appearance preference separate from technical support and surface functionality.

## Composition

One semantically coherent focal artefact; centred where suitable, not rigidly centred when reading order or interaction requires otherwise. Concentrate detail inside that artefact. Leave generous intentional negative space with a full-stage themed background. Keep controls peripheral, discoverable, interpretable and accessible. Minimal controls does not mean ambiguous unlabeled icons; accessible names, focus and optional accessible captions remain available.

## Materials and motion

Choose restrained materials, linework and palette fitting the subject. Structural blueprint reference: matte paper/eggshell, charcoal outlines, blue guides, restrained pastel room accents. Subtle light/shadow may add depth when appropriate; never mandatory effects. Use introduction/transformation to expose structure and continuity, then settle into inspection. Preserve the plan's ground plane and believable wall-height-to-footprint proportions. Occlusion must not hide what the user needs to understand.

## Text and speech

Prefer narration for explanatory prose, retain text that performs a necessary visual job: dimensions, diagram labels, mathematical notation, controls or requested copy. Keep captions/transcripts and non-audio access available. Do not silently delete useful content or safety-critical information. Audio support and speech synchronization require separate verification. Avoid generating dense prose panels as a substitute for audiovisual explanation.

## Selection

Suitable: buildings, mechanical/physical constructs, origami, one-object demonstrations, conversational presence. Not automatically suitable: websites, dashboards, document reading, multi-panel comparisons or tasks requiring dense meaningful notation. Infer from request and history; explicit preference overrides the default.

Conversational presence raises an unresolved contract issue: conversation/none currently prohibits authored visual mutation. A persistent host-owned avatar could provide presence without regenerating a program on every “hi”. Compare host-owned presence with explicitly authored visual greetings before changing the director schema. Do not insert unsolicited MS generation into all conversational turns.

## Evaluation

Assess focal clarity, spatial coherence, restraint, label necessity, control discoverability, accessible alternatives, gesture fidelity and stage continuity. Compare several domains and at least one counterexample website. Reference outcomes, not exact screenshot imitation. Keep the installed skill unchanged until matched trials support a generalizable change.
